**Chief of Staff**

Location: In-person, San Francisco Bay Area
Type: Full-time
Start Date: ASAP

**What You'll Own**

You will run the operating side of the business alongside the founders, and build the internal systems that scale with the research and teams. This is not an assistant role. You will have real authority and a direct line to the founders.

**What You Bring**

- Real business experience in an operational role at a technical company; fresh-out-of-school candidates are not a fit
- Familiarity with data centers, semiconductors, OEM/ODM, or adjacent infrastructure markets, enough to hold a partner-facing conversation without getting lost
- High agency, the kind that shows up when something needs to be figured out and nobody has told you it is your job
- Sharp writing, including the ability to draft investor updates and turn a rough partnership conversation into a clean two-page proposal
- Keen to use AI to automate functions, scale workflows, and support the broader team, without being 100% reliant upon it
- Good judgment about what to escalate and what to handle
- Comfort operating across strategy and execution, with the ability to run an investor update in the morning and a payroll issue in the afternoon
- Discretion: this role touches equity, comp, hiring decisions, board conversations, and partner negotiations

**Your Background / Bonus**

- Former Chief of Staff at a technical startup, ideally hardware or infrastructure adjacent
- Management consulting or investment banking with a specialty in semiconductors, data centers, or infrastructure work
- Private equity with a semiconductor, telecom, or infrastructure focus
- Partnerships at a chip company or data center operator
- Procurement experience across the data center stack
- Prior experience running the operational side of an early-stage company through a fundraise or major hiring push
